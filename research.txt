I'm making a new game called photowords. The game presents the player with 2 or more 
photos tagged with their names. Using contiguous letters from the tags of each, 
and a hint of the new word, the player is to guess the new word.
 For example the player is given three photos - 1. Uniform 2. Verse 3. City. 
 Also given a hint - "A place like Havard". 
 The player is to guess the word "University" with "Uni" from "Uniform", 
"vers" from "Verse" and "ity" from "City".
